## Support tickets

Customers open tickets and follow replies from their dashboard. Staff (support, moderator, admin and super admin) answer them from the support queue.

## Mail

Mail is a built-in inbox so you can message customers without leaving the platform.

- Staff can message **one user** or **broadcast to everyone**.
- Customers see an inbox with an **unread counter**, and can mark everything read or delete messages.
- Admins see every sent message with **read receipts**, such as `12/40 read`, grouped per broadcast.

## Announcements and status

Announcements are shown to customers on the site. The status page tracks incidents with timelines and scheduled maintenance.
